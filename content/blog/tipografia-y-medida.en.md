## Two families, one job each

Archivo carries the titles. It is wide, it stretches, and it holds large sizes without turning decorative. Raleway carries the body: neutral, with enough contrast for long paragraphs.

We did not pick them to “look designed.” We picked them because one orders and the other gets read.

## Measure

A paragraph longer than seventy-five characters per line gets skipped. On this site the body lives in a short column, even when the page is wide. Leftover space is not filled with text.

## What we do not do

We do not mix in a third family for “an accent.” If something needs emphasis, it changes weight or size inside the two families already in use.
