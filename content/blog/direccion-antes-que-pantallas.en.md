## The screen arrives late

When a project enters the studio, the first thing we ask for is not a moodboard. We ask for the problem: what a person must understand in the first seconds, and what decision they must be able to make.

Without that, every screen competes with the last one. Type changes, space changes, and the team ends up choosing by taste.

## What a direction is

A direction is an operational sentence and three visible decisions:

- What is read first.
- What can be acted on.
- What stays quiet.

With that, variants can be produced without reopening the project. If a proposal ignores those three, it is not a variant. It is another project.

## How it is handed off

The direction travels with the pieces. A loose file is not enough. We leave the rule written next to the component, so whoever continues does not have to guess why the title is larger than the button.
